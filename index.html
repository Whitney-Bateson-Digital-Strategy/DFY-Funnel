Module 3
Your Lead Magnet
🧲 What makes a great lead magnet? The best lead magnets give your ideal client a quick win — something that immediately helps them with a real problem — while making them feel empowered and confident that real progress is possible. When it's done right, your potential client walks away thinking, "If this free resource was this good, imagine what working with them would be like." Your paid offer becomes the obvious next step. For example, a nutritionist's "5-Day Gut Health Reset" guide helps someone feel noticeably better in less than a week — and naturally leads them to want personalized support to keep going.
